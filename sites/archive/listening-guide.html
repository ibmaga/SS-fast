Listening library

How to compare a transfer

Use the same playback level and listen to the same short passage in both versions.

What to listen for

Steady noise
Play a quiet gap between notes and listen for a constant background level.
Pitch drift
Use sustained or repeated notes to hear small changes in pitch without moving the playback position.
Short defects
Clicks have a sharp onset. Dropouts sound like a brief loss in level. Mark an approximate time before switching versions.
Source and limits
The examples use synthetic audio. The before tracks add a specific defect to each source sequence, and the after tracks use the aligned clean signal. Real transfers need a case-by-case assessment.
