Speed and track layout

Reel diameter helps describe the item. The recorded speed and track layout determine playback.

What to note before transfer

Playback speed
Check the box for inches per second or a duration note. Common label markings include 3¾ and 7½ ips.
Track layout
Note mono, stereo or multiple tracks when marked. A reel can hold more than one tracking arrangement.
Leader and winding
Keep the source in its own box and record which end is presented if the label specifies tails out.
