Microcassette

Recorder speed and quiet passages

Identify the recorder when possible and note the sides that contain material.

What to note before transfer

Source recorder
Use the notes on the shell or recorder to check whether the material uses 1.2 or 2.4 cm/s.
Quiet sections
Check the listening copy between speech or notes. A faint occupied passage can sound like a silent gap on a small speaker.
Mark interruptions
Flag pauses, repeats and unexpected interruptions if they matter to the sequence.
