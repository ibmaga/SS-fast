Shell, sides and levels

Write down the tape type and track order before timing the project.

What to note before transfer

Sides and order
Label sides A and B and note if the same material appears on both. The listed minutes should include only the sides you want transferred.
Noise reduction
Record any Dolby mark on the cassette or cover and keep that note with the transfer instructions.
Condition
A loose pressure pad, creased tape or damaged shell changes the handling required. Keep the recording in its case.
