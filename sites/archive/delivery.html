Transfer guide

From intake to delivery

Choose a preservation master, a listening copy or both. Keep a log with enough detail to understand the files later.

Preservation master

A lossless WAV transfer with the track order, source format and any observed damage documented.

Listening copy

A separate export with the requested noise and level treatment. Use MP3 for convenient playback or WAV for further editing.

Transfer notes

Keep the original filenames, source side, speed and any repairs together with the delivered files.
