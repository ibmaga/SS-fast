Before transfer

Prepare your recordings

A short checklist for identifying the media and capturing the instructions that affect the work.

Identify
List each tape with its label, format, approximate length and whether both sides matter.
Inspect
Photograph damage or debris on the outside. Set aside any item with visible mould or a loose reel.
Specify
Note which tracks to keep, whether to preserve pauses and what the listening copy will be used for.
Estimate
Add a separate line to the estimate whenever format, duration or restoration options differ.
