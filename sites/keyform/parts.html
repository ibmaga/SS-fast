Workshop notes

How parts fit

The configurator uses reference parts. Check real hardware against the manufacturer’s drawing before ordering.

Case & mount

Match the mounting system and exact board family. Two cases with the same key count can use different hardware.

Layout & plate

ANSI and ISO use different key openings. Match the plate to the PCB family and the layout you plan to type on.

Switches & stabilizers

Switch technology, pin count and stabilizer mounting determine what fits the selected PCB and plate.
