Studio notes

From daylight to detail

A lighting plan starts with the people who use the space and the surfaces that shape what they see.

Read the room

Record daylight at different hours. Note entrances, key tasks and surfaces that reflect or absorb light.

Build the lighting layers

Use separate layers for orientation, work and emphasis. Sketch where each circuit is needed and how it will be dimmed.

Test at full scale

Mock up a small area at the planned mounting height. Check for glare from seated and standing positions.

Hand over the controls

Document fixture positions and scene settings. Leave a simple control schedule that people can use without the drawings.
