Racecraft

Driver guide

A short briefing for joining a lobby, taking the start and keeping a race clean.

Before joining

Calibrate brake and steering inputs, map your pit limiter and confirm you can see relative timing.

Qualifying and starts

Leave a gap before a timed lap. On a rolling start, hold your lane and follow the pace from the lobby briefing.

Passing and defending

Commit early enough for the other driver to see you. Leave a car width at the edge when another car is alongside.

Incidents and rejoining

Hold your brakes after a spin. Check mirrors and relative timing before rejoining so the next group can pass safely.

Driver swaps

Agree on the changeover lap, tyre strategy and how to confirm the next driver is connected.
