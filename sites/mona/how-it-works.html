How it works

Sending a file takes less time than writing the email about it.

Upload

Drag files into the page or pick them from your device. Folders are zipped automatically.

Set the rules

Choose an expiry, an optional passcode and how many times the link may be opened.

Share the link

Send the link by any channel. You can revoke it from your dashboard at any moment.

What happens to your files

Files are encrypted in transit and at rest. When a link expires or reaches its download limit, the file is removed from storage and cannot be recovered.

Recipients

Anyone with the link can download without creating an account. If you set a passcode, share it separately from the link.
