Privacy

We keep only what is needed to deliver a transfer: the file, its expiry and download settings, and basic technical logs used to prevent abuse.

Files

Logs

Server logs are kept for a short period for security and reliability, then removed.

Cookies

We use only the cookies required for signing in. We do not run third-party advertising trackers.
